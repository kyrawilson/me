## BERT Shows Garden Path Effects

### EACL 2023

tl;dr: We compare various BERT models' performance to humans' in extracting information from garden path sentences. All the models perform have 
relatively similar performance on a downstream question answering task, which is lower than human performance. A probe task reveals semantic roles 
are not being assigned correctly, and internal representations are not consistent between models.
